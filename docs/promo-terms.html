Promo Code Terms

GallerySync · for testers and anyone given a GallerySync promo code

A GallerySync promo code unlocks GallerySync Pro for free, for good. It is a thank-you from the developer and is redeemed through Google Play.

What the code gives you

  • GallerySync Pro, the one-time unlock that adds every Cloud beyond the first (Google Drive, Google Photos, Dropbox, IDrive e2 and Backblaze B2 alongside your free Cloud).
  • It is permanent. It does not expire once redeemed and carries on into later versions of the app, including the public release.
  • It is tied to the Google account you redeem it with, like any Google Play purchase, so it follows you to a new phone signed in to the same account.

Redeeming it

  • In the app: Settings → Unlock Pro → Redeem code, then enter the code.
  • Or in the Play Store app: your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code.
  • Redemption is handled by Google Play and is subject to Google Play's Terms of Service.

The rules

  • One redemption per Google account.
  • A code shared with a group can be redeemed only a limited number of times. Once that number is reached it stops working, so please do not pass it on to anyone outside the group.
  • Codes have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and must not be sold or traded.
  • A code must be redeemed by the date given when it was sent. After that date it no longer works.
  • Codes are only for people old enough to use GallerySync, in countries where it is available on Google Play.
  • Google may change or end its promo code programme at any time, which could stop an unredeemed code from working. A code already redeemed is not affected by the code's expiry.

Your data

When you redeem a code, the GallerySync app learns only that Pro is unlocked on your Google account; it collects nothing else. Google Play keeps its own record of the redemption under its own privacy policy. See the privacy policy for how GallerySync handles data.

Contact

Problems with a code can be raised at github.com/iant8055/GallerySync/issues.
