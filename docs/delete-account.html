Delete your account and data

Gallery Sync

Gallery Sync does not create an account for you. You sign in with your existing Microsoft account, and Gallery Sync only holds a sign-in token on your own device. There is no Gallery Sync server, and no copy of your data is stored anywhere by us.

How to remove your data

  1. Sign out in the app. Open Gallery Sync and tap Sign out. This immediately deletes the stored sign-in token and the local index of your files.
  2. Revoke Gallery Sync's access to your Microsoft account. Go to account.live.com/consent/Manage, find Gallery Sync in the list, and choose Remove these permissions. Gallery Sync can no longer reach your OneDrive after this.
  3. Uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes every remaining file Gallery Sync created on your device, including its local database and any cached media.

Completing step 1 or step 2 alone is enough to cut off access. Step 3 clears the last of the local data.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhere it livesWhat happens
Microsoft sign-in token Encrypted storage on your device Deleted when you sign out or uninstall. Retention period: none.
Index of your OneDrive file names and folders A database on your device Deleted when you sign out or uninstall. Retention period: none.
Cached photos and videos Gallery Sync's private storage on your device Deleted when you uninstall. Retention period: none.
Your photos and videos in OneDrive Your Microsoft account Kept. They belong to you. Gallery Sync never deletes them.

Gallery Sync never deletes anything from your OneDrive. Removing the app does not remove your photos. To delete files stored in OneDrive, use OneDrive itself, or close your Microsoft account at account.microsoft.com.

Data held on our servers

None. Gallery Sync has no server or backend. It talks only to Microsoft's OneDrive service using your own account. We do not receive, store, or process your photos, your file names, or your account details, so there is nothing on our side to delete and no retention period to report.

Need help?

If you cannot complete these steps or want to confirm your data has been removed, open an issue at github.com/iant8055/GallerySync/issues.
