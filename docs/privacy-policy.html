Privacy Policy

Gallery Sync — last updated 17 August 2026

The short version. Gallery Sync sends your photos and videos to your own Microsoft OneDrive account and nowhere else. The developer operates no server, receives none of your data, and cannot see your photos, your file names, or your account. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app does with your data

Gallery Sync backs up photos and videos from your device to your own OneDrive account, and lets you browse what is stored there. To do that it needs to read the media on your device and to sign in to your Microsoft account.

Your photos travel from your device directly to Microsoft's OneDrive service over an encrypted connection. They do not pass through any server belonging to the developer, because no such server exists.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Gallery Sync has no backend service. The developer does not receive, store, or process your photos, your file names, your email address, your device identifiers, or any usage information. There is no account to register with the developer and no data held about you anywhere.

Third parties

Gallery Sync communicates with Microsoft OneDrive, using the Microsoft account you sign in with. Microsoft's handling of the data you store with them is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Signing in uses Microsoft's official authentication library. Your password is never seen by Gallery Sync — you enter it on Microsoft's own sign-in page, and the app only ever receives a token that grants access to your files.

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no other third-party service that collects or transmits your data.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos — to read the media you have chosen to back up. The app only reads files; it never deletes them.
  • Internet and network state — to upload to OneDrive, and to wait for a suitable connection before doing so.

You choose which albums are backed up. Albums you exclude are never read or uploaded.

Storage on your device

Gallery Sync keeps a local database describing your OneDrive contents, and may cache copies of files you open so they can be used by other apps. This data lives in the app's private storage, is not readable by other apps, and is protected by your device's encryption.

Your photos are never deleted

Gallery Sync does not delete anything from your OneDrive, and does not delete the original photos on your device. It only ever adds copies to your cloud storage and manages its own local cache.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time, which removes the stored token and local index. Uninstalling removes everything else the app created. Full instructions, including how to revoke the app's access to your Microsoft account, are on the account and data deletion page.

Children

Gallery Sync is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. As stated above, the developer collects no information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how Gallery Sync handles your data, can be raised at github.com/iant8055/GallerySync/issues.
