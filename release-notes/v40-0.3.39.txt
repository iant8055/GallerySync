<en-US>
• Sync is now called Optimize, and Sync now is Back Up Now.
• Albums show in red how many files their Cloud no longer has. Select them on the album's page and press Resend.
• A folder inside DCIM can go to its own Cloud.
• Restore lists every Cloud's folders, named with their Cloud, and only files the Cloud still holds.
• After updating, choose Optimize photos and Optimize video in Settings again.
</en-US>
