<en-US>
• Albums backed up to Dropbox, Google Drive, pCloud, Backblaze B2 or IDrive e2 now say "verified", like OneDrive, once the Cloud confirms each file at the right size.
• The upload line names the album and shows the size sent of the file's total, and Hide Details now hides it too.
</en-US>
